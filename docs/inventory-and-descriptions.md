# Inventory corrections (1.9.1)

- Repair kit: choose a character (Self by default), then damaged armor/shield from that character's inventory. Stored armor can be repaired too. One item is repaired per use; a stack is split to preserve the condition of the remaining units. The item gains critical durability up to its maximum. Equipped armor updates the body part's combined HP; repair never heals biological HP or implants.
- Campaign pool: shared room metadata persists across character/scene switches. Reads are ordered and metadata events supersede older reads. Opening the pool waits for an active catalog scan. Scan fingerprints are recorded only after persistence succeeds; renders also schedule scans of existing scene items.
- Descriptions: compact info buttons on inventory/pool items, skills and perks open a text-only dialog, accessible to players. Item descriptions are edited in their usual item form. Skill/perk descriptions are edited with the skill pencil, retained in templates/JSON, and do not trigger rolls.
- Resources and implants: range relief controls are removed; previously stored relief is ignored and removed on inventory normalization. Attribute/skill bonuses and weapon/perk range relief remain available.
- Unknown skill bonuses: an active item can display an absent skill at level 0 without adding the numeric bonus. It does not create a permanent learned skill. When the character has a learned skill (including level 0), the bonus applies. Matching supports internal skill keys and visible labels. Removing the item removes its temporary skill entry.
